---
title: Jobs
description: How locks pause queues and interrupt jobs with @10x-media/jobs.
---

A lock blocks writes from jobs like from anything else, so a job that writes into locked content fails on that write, with or without the jobs plugin. With [`@10x-media/jobs`](/jobs) installed, content-lock also behaves well towards them; it detects the plugin and needs no setup.

## What happens

- **A lock on everything pauses every queue.** The plugin's worker and `queue-run` endpoint claim no new jobs until it ends. Jobs already queued simply wait.
- **A partial lock keeps queues running.** Jobs that do not touch the frozen content carry on; one that writes into it is interrupted.
- **An interrupted job** fails for good by default, with "Interrupted by content-lock" and no retries. A job marked with `deferOnInterrupt` goes back to the queue until the lock ends instead, without spending a retry. See [interruptions](/jobs/interruptions).
- **Ending or changing a window** releases the jobs it deferred right away, and only those: **End now** on a lock over users does not wake jobs waiting on a separate lock over posts. A window without a known end defers its jobs for a day; ending it releases them sooner. A window deleted directly in the database releases its jobs within about a minute.
- **If the lock state cannot be read**, writes are rejected (see [timing](/content-lock/enforcement#timing)) and every queue pauses until it can. A job marked with `deferOnInterrupt` that is stopped meanwhile defers for one minute, since no window is known that could release it, and runs again once the state can be read.

Only the plugin's worker and `queue-run` honour the pause. Payload's `autoRun` and `/api/payload-jobs/run` keep claiming jobs, which then fail or defer on their writes.

## Options

```ts
contentLock({
  jobs: { queues: ['catalog-sync'] }, // also pause these queues during partial locks
})
```

`jobs: false` turns the integration off.

## Writing lock-friendly jobs

- Mark jobs that are safe to run again from the start with `deferOnInterrupt`. Workflows re-run from the step they stopped at.
- Long jobs can call `checkpoint({ job, req })` between batches to stop cleanly when their queue is paused.
- Jobs that would rather skip locked work than fail check `isContentLocked(payload, { collection })` first.
- Jobs that catch write errors per item, or pay for an external call before they write, call `assertContentUnlocked(req, { collection })` first. A lock caught per item never reaches the jobs plugin; asserted up front, it defers or fails the job before any work is done.
- Jobs that write through `payload.db` or the raw driver are not stopped by the lock at all; only a paused queue keeps them out, so check `isContentLocked` in them.
