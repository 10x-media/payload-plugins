---
title: Privacy
description: What a recording contains, where it goes, and what to tell the people using it.
---

A screen recording contains whatever was on the screen. In a CMS that can include personal data: customer records, form submissions, user accounts.

- **Recorded:** the shared screen, window or tab; the microphone; the camera, only if switched on.
- **Sent with it:** the editor's name, email and role in this CMS, the page they started on, the browser, language, time zone and screen size, and the site's version. Console errors only with `captureConsole: true`.
- **Where it goes:** the video to Bunny Stream (EU), everything else to the 10x Media system. The recording is visible to the 10x Media team and, once encoded, in the client's own portal.
- **Retention:** the 10x system's clip retention applies.

The drawer tells editors what is recorded before they start. Mention the recorder in the data processing agreement with the client, and ask editors to show only what they are allowed to share. Restrict who sees the recorder with `access`.
