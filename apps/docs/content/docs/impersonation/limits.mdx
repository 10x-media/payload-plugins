---
title: Limits
description: What v1 does not cover.
---

Out of scope for v1:

- Read-only impersonation
- GraphQL mutations
- A shipped frontend React banner (`getImpersonation` is the helper)
- Preference isolation hooks
- Email kill-switch
- Step-up password
- Notify-the-target emails
- BroadcastChannel multi-tab
- Automatic audit-log attribution (`@10x-media/audit-logs` can read `_impersonation`)

The admin collection cannot use `useSessions: false` or `disableLocalStrategy`. Numeric ids (Postgres) are accepted on start and terminate.

Locked (`lockUntil`) targets are allowed and stamped. Trash, unverified-if-verify, self, nesting, and API-key callers are refused.
